Objective

Analyze two years of e-commerce order data to identify revenue drivers, regional performance gaps, and growth opportunities for business planning.

Revenue Trend

Revenue shows strong seasonality: Q4 (holiday season) generated 32% of total revenue. Year-over-year revenue grew -6.0%, indicating healthy demand expansion.

Regional Performance

Northeast leads all regions. The gap between the top and bottom region suggests an opportunity to replicate best practices from high-performing territories.

Category Mix

Furniture drives the most revenue, while Clothing delivers the highest profit margin — a classic volume-vs-margin tradeoff worth balancing in promotions.

Sales Team

R. Hassan is the top performer at $765K. Coaching the bottom quartile up to the team median would add an estimated $33K in revenue.

Key Findings

  • Q4 seasonality is extreme (32% of revenue) — inventory and staffing plans must peak in October.
  • Northeast outperforms; investigate its playbook for rollout to lagging regions.
  • Clothing has the best margin profile — prioritize it in marketing spend.
  • YoY growth of -6.0% is solid; sustaining it requires diversifying beyond the Q4 spike.

Recommendations

1. Smooth seasonality: run mid-year promotions on Clothing to lift the slow Q1/Q2 periods.
2. Regional playbook: document Northeast's tactics and pilot them in the lowest-performing region.
3. Rep coaching program: pair bottom-quartile reps with R. Hassan to close the performance gap.

Methodology

Data cleaned and analyzed in Python (pandas). Metrics: revenue, profit, margin, AOV, YoY growth. Visualizations built with matplotlib. Dataset: synthetic e-commerce orders generated for portfolio demonstration.
